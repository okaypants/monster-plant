# Tarot cards

The 78 card pictures are the Rider–Waite–Smith tarot, drawn by Pamela Colman Smith and published by William Rider & Son in
1909 (public domain), from the scans of the 1909 printing on Wikimedia Commons ("Category:Rider-Waite tarot deck (Roses &
Lilies)", files "RWS1909 - …"). Each was cropped right at its printed black frame (so every card sits the same way),
white-balanced so the yellowed paper reads as clean white, made a little brighter and more colorful, shrunk to 72×124 and
reduced to 24 colors for the room's pixel style (Jackie, Nov 16: brighter, recentered, more modern). File names: M00–M21 the Major Arcana (Waite's numbering: Strength 8, Justice 11); W, C, S, P for Wands, Cups,
Swords and Pentacles, 01 the Ace to 10, then 11 Page, 12 Knight, 13 Queen, 14 King.

back.png is drawn for this game.

The meanings in js/tarotdata.js are from A. E. Waite, *The Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1910, public domain), via Wikisource.
