# Book covers

The twelve book covers were made by Jackie as one sheet (`covers_sheet.png`, Nov 17) and cut into one picture per book,
42 x 60 pixels, 24 colors each. Left to right, top to bottom on the sheet: fairy, almanac, nonsense, grimoire, songs, stars,
riddles (now the Cats book, still with the keyhole cover), proverbs, letters, recipes, ghost, field (the book ids in `js/care.js`).

To change a cover, replace its picture here with one of the same size.

Six more covers came as a second sheet (`covers_sheet2.png`, Nov 27), cut the same way by `tools/covers_cut.py`. Left to right,
top to bottom: moderns, detective, poems, odd, small, and a sleeping cat for riddles (the Cats book), which replaces the keyhole.

The moderns, Detective stories and Odd novels were taken out of the game the day they went in (Nov 27), so their covers (moderns, detective, odd) are not kept here; they are still on `covers_sheet2.png`.
