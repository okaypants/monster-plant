# Data

cities.json: every city in the world with more than 15,000 people (34,216), with its region, country, position and time
zone, from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org), cities15000, countryInfo and admin1CodesASCII, downloaded Nov 16, 2026.
GeoNames data is licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Used by the birth charts
(js/charts.js) to find the city you were born in and the place you play; loaded only when the charts are opened.
